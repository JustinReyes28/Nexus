## 8. Email Notifications (Google SMTP + Nodemailer)

**Transactional Emails**

- Welcome email with getting started guide
- Email verification on sign-up
- Password reset instructions
- Invitation emails for team collaboration

**Reminder Emails**

- Upcoming deadline warnings at 7, 3, 1 days
- Overdue task notifications
- Weekly progress summaries
- Milestone completion celebrations

**Collaboration Emails**

- New team member joined notifications
- Comments and feedback alerts
- Document shared notifications
- Advisor response alerts

**Email Design**

- Responsive HTML templates
- Branded header with Nexus logo
- Clear CTAs linking back to platform
- Unsubscribe options for non-critical emails
